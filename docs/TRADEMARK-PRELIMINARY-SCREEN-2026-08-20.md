# Preliminary trademark screen: `Eclipse DnD Forge`

**Search date:** 2026-08-20  
**Scope:** preliminary word-mark screening only; Nice Classes 9, 41, and 42  
**Decision:** `NOT CLEARED` — keep the product-name commercial/billing gate closed

This document is an engineering risk record, not a legal opinion, availability
opinion, freedom-to-operate opinion, or application instruction. No filing,
opposition, contact with an owner, or other legal action was performed.

## Query set

Classes 9 (software/game software), 41 (game, entertainment, and education
services), and 42 (SaaS, hosted software, and software development) were used as
the initial scope. The final goods/services wording can change the comparison,
and a class number alone does not decide likelihood of confusion.

The following word queries and Class 9/41/42 filters were prepared for each
official portal where supported:

1. Exact: `Eclipse DnD Forge`
2. Near-exact: `Eclipse Forge`, `Eclipse D&D Forge`
3. Components: `Eclipse` AND `DnD` AND `Forge`
4. Variants: `D&D`, `DND`, `Dungeons & Dragons`, `Dungeons and Dragons`
5. Russian variants: `ЭКЛИПС ДНД ФОРДЖ`, `ЭКЛИПС ФОРДЖ`, `ДНД`, `Д&Д`

USPTO syntax recorded for reproducibility:

```text
CM:"eclipse dnd forge"
FM:"eclipse dnd forge"
CM:"eclipse forge" AND (IC:009 OR IC:041 OR IC:042)
CM:(/.*eclipse.*/ AND /.*dnd.*/ AND /.*forge.*/) AND (IC:009 OR IC:041 OR IC:042)
CM:(/.*dnd.*/ OR /.*dungeons.*dragons.*/) AND (IC:009 OR IC:041 OR IC:042)
```

USPTO says an exact-wording knock-out search is only the first step and recommends
expanded wording, spelling/pronunciation, and coordinated-class searches in its
[official search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching).

## Official-source evidence

### WIPO Global Brand Database

- [Official portal](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database)
- [Official coverage and limitations FAQ](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/faqs_branddb)
- **Result: inconclusive.** The accessible search surface presented an ALTCHA
  challenge and did not expose an auditable result list in this session. WIPO
  prohibits automated querying, notes that national update frequency depends on
  the contributing office, and recommends national/regional searches and a
  trademark attorney. No exact-absence or availability claim is made.

### EUIPO

- [EUIPO eSearch plus](https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/)
- [Official eSearch FAQ](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/help-centre/searches/faq-esearch-plus)
- **Result table: inconclusive.** The dynamic interface was reachable, but its
  auditable result records were unavailable through the accessible channel.
- Material related evidence: EUIPO's official article
  [Game on: Navigating intellectual property in esports](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/news/game-on-navigating-intellectual-property-in-esports)
  reports that the General Court upheld likelihood of confusion between
  `DUNGEONS` and the earlier EU word mark `DUNGEONS & DRAGONS` for identical or
  similar goods/services in Classes 9, 28, and 41 (Case T-700/18). This does not
  decide the proposed name, but it is directly relevant to a D&D-adjacent element
  used for game/software services.

### USPTO

- [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/)
- **Exact-result extraction: inconclusive.** The official frontend was reachable,
  but its interactive client could not be operated reliably in this environment;
  therefore no zero-hit claim is recorded.
- An official USPTO TTAB record lists Wizards of the Coast LLC's associated
  registrations including `D&D` (U.S. Reg. No. 1,779,033), multiple
  `DUNGEONS & DRAGONS` registrations, `DUNGEONS & DRAGONS ONLINE`, and
  `D&D BEYOND`: [official TTAB record](https://ttabvue.uspto.gov/ttabvue/ttabvue-91298616-OPP-4.pdf).
- That record also shows proceedings involving `DNDDICE` and `DICEDND`. This is
  not a merits opinion for the proposed name, but it is evidence that `DnD`-form
  variants cannot safely be dismissed as merely descriptive shorthand.
- `ECLIPSE` also requires a professional crowded-field/family analysis. This
  screen did not determine which potentially relevant records are live,
  enforceable, or sufficiently close today.

### Rospatent / FIPS

## Preliminary risk assessment

| Element | Preliminary signal | Engineering treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Full phrase | No auditable exact-result set was obtained from all four systems. | `inconclusive`; never treat as available. |
| `DnD` / `D&D` | Material registered-rights and enforcement evidence exists; EUIPO reports a confusion finding in overlapping game/software classes. | `high risk`; block billing, paid distribution, and commercial marketing. |
| `Eclipse` | Common/crowded-looking component whose current status and scope were not fully resolved. | `review required`; run owner/status and goods/services analysis. |
| `Forge` | Suggestive/common component that does not neutralize the other elements. | Do not rely on it as a clearance distinction. |

The outcome is not that infringement is proven. The evidence is sufficient to
reject self-clearance and keep the existing commercial gate closed.

## Limitations

This screen does not conclusively cover unregistered/common-law rights, company
names, domains, app stores, social handles, game marketplaces, newly filed or
unpublished applications, database lag, assignments, expired rights with residual
reputation, logos/trade dress, phonetic/conceptual/transliteration similarity,
family-of-marks analysis, final goods/services, consumers, trade channels,
territories, fame, agreements, or possible defences.

## Required next step

Before billing, paid distribution, commercial marketing, or filing under this
name, instruct a qualified trademark/patent attorney covering the US, EU, and
Russia to:

1. preserve professional exact, phonetic, conceptual, transliteration, owner, and
   family-of-marks searches in WIPO, EUIPO/TMview, USPTO, FIPS, and relevant
   national/common-law sources;
2. verify live/dead status, ownership, prosecution history, Nice classes, exact
   goods/services, and trade channels for the closest `D&D`/`DnD`, `ECLIPSE`,
   `ECLIPSE FORGE`, and `FORGE` records;
3. assess the name against Wizards of the Coast's portfolio and enforcement
   history, including whether spelling `DnD` instead of `D&D` changes risk;
4. provide a written clearance opinion and, if needed, screen original replacement
   names before public adoption; and
5. only after that written decision, advise on territories and filing scope.

Until the written decision is attached to the product legal record, the
trademark-clearance field remains false and commercial use remains blocked.
